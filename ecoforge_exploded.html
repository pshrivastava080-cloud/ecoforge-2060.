SCIENCE BEHIND ECOFORGE

One Machine. Multiple Principles.

01 — FILTRATION

Air passes through filter material that can capture suspended particles. The physical model can demonstrate airflow and filtration.

02 — ADSORPTION

Activated carbon has a large surface area and can adsorb certain gases and odor molecules.

03 — CARBON CAPTURE

Advanced sorbents can capture CO₂. This is represented as a future integrated stage.

04 — RENEWABLE ENERGY

Future EcoForge systems could use renewable electricity to power advanced processing technologies.

05 — RESOURCE RECOVERY

The long-term concept is to turn recovered carbon and waste streams into useful materials.

06 — CIRCULAR ECONOMY

The goal is a system where resources are recovered, reused and kept in circulation.
